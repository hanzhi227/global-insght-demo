# OPS-002 — AX-210 Line Changeover and Batch Traceability

**Northstar Precision Components — Cedar Falls Plant**  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01 | **Revision:** 3  
**Owner:** Production Operations Manager | **Approval role:** Plant Manager  
**Category:** Operations | **Controlled record:** OPS-002-F1 Changeover and Genealogy Record

**Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.**

## 1. Purpose and operating boundary

This procedure controls production sequencing, line clearance, container identity, and batch genealogy for AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets at Cedar Falls Plant. It covers a change of work order, incoming aluminum lot, routing revision, or other batch-defining condition identified by the approved traveler. It applies around conveyor C-12 and hydraulic press HP-4, with air dryer AD-2 and coolant CL-7 recorded where relevant to the work order.

Operations coordinates the changeover and material movements. Maintenance owns equipment checks and setup verification under MNT-001. Safety owns isolation authorization and permits under SAF-001. Quality owns first-production acceptance, holds, and release under QUA-001. This document does not prescribe their technical limits.

No guards may be bypassed. Isolated equipment must not restart without authorized release. Records must not be falsified. Only Quality may release held product. The same controls apply during trial pieces, schedule recovery, and short production runs.

## 2. Roles and preparation

The Shift Lead confirms the dispatch sequence and names the next AX-210 work order. The Material Coordinator stages the incoming lot and removes material no longer assigned to the cell. The Cell Operator maintains container identity and records actual transfers. Maintenance and Quality provide their respective documented decisions. The lead assembles those references but does not sign on their behalf.

Prepare the changeover packet at least 30 minutes before the planned stop. Include the outgoing and incoming work orders, traveler revisions, incoming material lot, expected container count, available staging location, and required supporting authorizations. If the packet is incomplete, revise the start forecast rather than begin with undocumented material.

For interruptions and revised dispatch priorities, use OPS-001. Physical staging and shipment transfer use OPS-003. These documents govern the scheduling and movement interfaces without changing batch identity requirements.

## 3. Close the outgoing batch

Stop feeding the outgoing lot at the approved transition point. Identify all remaining blanks, formed brackets, scrap awaiting disposition, and product awaiting Quality review. Separate the last outgoing container from the first incoming container; never use a shared container to bridge batches.

Record quantities in four distinct categories: unused blanks, processed pieces, documented scrap, and unresolved quantity. Reconcile these categories to issued material before closing the outgoing traveler. The permitted unexplained quantity difference is zero. If reconciliation fails, leave the batch open, segregate the affected containers, and notify the Shift Lead and Quality.

The operator and lead conduct a joint line-clearance review of feed locations, discharge locations, temporary accumulation points, and traveler holders. This is an identification and material-removal check, not an equipment inspection. Equipment access and intervention must follow the owning procedures.

## 4. Establish the incoming batch

Use an Operations batch identifier in the form AX210-YYYYMMDD-NNN, where the date is the batch-opening date and NNN is the plant-controlled daily sequence. A batch identifier must not be reused, including after cancellation. Record the associated work order, aluminum lot, traveler revision, and opening time.

Assign every container a unique suffix, such as C001, within its batch. Production containers may contain no more than 120 brackets and may contain only one batch. A partially filled container remains uniquely identified and carries its actual count; rounding to nominal capacity is prohibited.

The container label must show part number, batch identifier, container suffix, quantity, material lot, current status, and destination. Retain the CL-7 supply-lot association and any replacement event in the genealogy record when coolant is used. A coolant change does not automatically create a new product batch; Quality determines any product implications through QUA-001.

## 5. Changeover completion and first production

The Shift Lead verifies that outgoing material has a recorded destination and incoming material matches the packet. Record Maintenance's setup-check reference and any applicable Safety release reference. If either required decision is absent, do not start the affected operation.

Identify initial production as awaiting Quality review. Keep it segregated from released product while Quality performs the applicable acceptance process. Operations may schedule subsequent activity only within the permissions documented by Quality; this procedure grants no provisional product release.

Record the first incoming container identifier and actual start time. The changeover record must distinguish “material ready,” “equipment available,” “production started,” and “product released.” Combining these milestones into one completion timestamp conceals dependencies and is not permitted.

## 6. Movement, splitting, and exceptions

Record each container transfer within 5 minutes of the physical move. Capture source, destination, time, quantity, status, and responsible role. A split receives new container suffixes linked to the parent; record the parent as emptied or retain its remaining actual quantity. Never merge different batches.

A missing label, unreadable identifier, or suspected mixed container triggers segregation and Quality notification. Do not reconstruct identity from memory. Supporting records may inform Quality's disposition but do not independently authorize use. For electronic outages, maintain the same fields on controlled paper and reconcile before the next handover acceptance.

## 7. Records, measures, and revision history

Retain the packet, clearance checks, genealogy, movement records, and authorization references with the work order. Operations reviews compliance weekly: 100% uniquely identified containers, zero unexplained reconciliation differences at batch closure, and 100% transfers recorded within 5 minutes. Exceptions remain visible until resolved.

**Revision history:** Revision 1 established batch travelers. Revision 2 added parent-child container linkage. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, establishes the 120-piece container limit, explicit milestone separation, and timed movement recording.
