# OPS-001 — Shift Handover and Production Escalation

**Northstar Precision Components — Cedar Falls Plant**  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01 | **Revision:** 3  
**Owner:** Operations Manager | **Approval role:** Plant Manager  
**Category:** Operations | **Controlled record:** OPS-001-F1 Shift Continuity Log

**Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.**

## 1. Purpose and authority

This procedure controls shift turnover, production priorities, and escalation for AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets. It applies to the production cell using conveyor C-12, hydraulic press HP-4, and air dryer AD-2, including work involving coolant CL-7. Operations owns scheduling and material flow. Safety owns isolation authorization and permits. Maintenance owns equipment checks. Quality owns product disposition and release. A delivery commitment never transfers those authorities to Operations.

No employee may bypass guards, restart isolated equipment without authorized release, falsify records, or release held product without Quality authorization. If schedule recovery conflicts with those requirements, the schedule must change. Safety and equipment requirements are referenced through SAF-001 and MNT-001; product acceptance and release are referenced through QUA-001. Their numerical requirements are not reproduced here.

## 2. Roles and handover window

The outgoing Shift Lead prepares the continuity log and conducts the handover. The incoming Shift Lead accepts production responsibility after reviewing unresolved issues. The Cell Operator verifies actual work-in-process location and reports discrepancies. The Material Coordinator reconciles staging lanes. The Operations Manager decides scheduling conflicts and authorizes revised production priorities, not technical release.

The formal overlap occupies the final 15 minutes of the outgoing shift. The outgoing lead updates the log before that window begins. If the incoming lead is unavailable, the Operations Manager appoints a qualified acting lead. Until acceptance is recorded, the outgoing lead retains coordination responsibility; unattended production is not an acceptable substitute for handover.

## 3. Required continuity information

Record the date, shift identifier, outgoing and incoming roles, active AX-210 work order, batch identifiers, scheduled quantity, completed quantity, and remaining quantity. Separate completed accepted stock from product awaiting review. Do not describe either category simply as “finished.”

For C-12, HP-4, and AD-2, identify whether each asset is running, stopped, awaiting Maintenance, or isolated. Cite the governing ticket or authorization reference without interpreting its technical adequacy. For CL-7, record the production batch association and any open concern; Maintenance and Quality determine the applicable checks and product implications.

List every open hold, temporary staging assignment, unavailable material, incomplete changeover, and shipment deadline. Each unresolved item needs an accountable role, next action, and next update time. Attach the current dispatch sequence rather than relying on a whiteboard photograph alone.

## 4. Joint verification and acceptance

Both leads compare the log with the cell status board and walk the designated production and staging locations. Verify that the batch travelers correspond to the containers present. Confirm that isolated or held areas remain visibly identified. Do not remove a tag or move held stock merely to simplify the walkdown.

Resolve quantity differences against the batch movement record described in OPS-002. If an exact count is unavailable, identify the quantity as unverified, segregate the affected material, and assign reconciliation. An estimate must never be entered as a confirmed count.

The incoming lead records acceptance, exceptions, and the handover completion time. Any unresolved discrepancy affecting batch identity, product hold status, or isolation status blocks production on the affected work. Unaffected work may continue only when its separation is demonstrable and its existing authorizations remain valid.

## 5. Production escalation ladder

Start a downtime event when the scheduled operation stops unexpectedly. Notify the Shift Lead immediately for any safety concern, suspected mixed batch, uncertain isolation status, or missing hold identification. The lead engages Safety, Maintenance, or Quality according to ownership; there is no waiting period for these events.

For a scheduling or material-flow interruption without those concerns, the lead attempts coordination locally. At 10 minutes of unresolved interruption, notify the Operations Manager and record the reason. At 30 minutes, the manager publishes a recovery decision: resequence approved work, obtain material support, or revise the delivery forecast. During an unresolved event, update the continuity log at least every 30 minutes.

If the forecast threatens a shipment commitment, notify the Shipping Coordinator immediately after the forecast changes. Shipment staging and release follow OPS-003. A forecast is not a release authorization, and overtime does not substitute for required changeover completion.

## 6. Recovery and exceptions

Before resuming affected work, the lead checks that the appropriate owning function has documented its decision. Maintenance completion alone does not release product; Quality release alone does not authorize restarting isolated equipment. Apply both controls when both conditions exist.

For an unavailable electronic log, use the controlled paper form, preserving actual event times and the time of later transcription. Cross-reference the paper record after system restoration. Correct an inaccurate entry transparently with reason and author role; never erase the event history.

If a handover is interrupted by an emergency, stabilize the area under the governing safety procedure and resume the review afterward. Record the interruption and identify which items remain unverified.

## 7. Records, performance, and revision history

Store the continuity log, dispatch revisions, downtime references, and acceptance entries in the work-order record set. Operations reviews handover performance weekly. Targets are 100% documented shift acceptance, 100% unresolved items assigned to an owner, and 100% required escalation notifications recorded within their specified windows. Missed targets require an action owner and due date, not retrospective alteration of timestamps.

**Revision history:** Revision 1 established the continuity log. Revision 2 added explicit held-stock and staging reconciliation. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, separates scheduling recovery from technical authorization and adds the 10-minute escalation, 30-minute recovery decision, and recurring update requirements.
