# QUA-003 — Gauge Calibration and Measurement Traceability

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01  
**Revision:** 3  
**Owner role:** Quality Metrology Coordinator  
**Approval role:** Quality Manager  
**Controlled records:** QF-301 Gauge Register; QF-302 Verification Log; QF-303 Calibration Impact Review

## 1. Purpose and responsibilities

This procedure controls instruments used to accept AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets at Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant. It covers digital calipers, pin gauges, angle fixtures, burr comparators, and their reference standards. Quality owns measurement suitability and product release. Maintenance owns equipment checks; Operations owns scheduling and material flow; Safety owns isolation authorization and permits. Never bypass guards, restart isolated equipment without authorized release, or falsify calibration or inspection records. Only Quality may release held product.

## 2. Registration and calibration intervals

The Metrology Coordinator assigns a unique gauge identifier before use and records type, range, resolution, location, calibration provider, certificate reference, last calibration, and due date in QF-301. Each label shows identifier, due date, and status. Instruments used only for setup are labeled NOT FOR ACCEPTANCE and cannot provide release evidence. Loaned or replacement instruments meet the same registration requirements as owned instruments.

Calibrate digital calipers and angle fixtures every six months, pin gauges every 12 months, and burr comparators every 12 months. Reference standards require calibration every 12 months. A gauge is overdue at the start of the day following its labeled due date. There is no grace period. The coordinator schedules replacement before that date; Operations reschedules inspection if no qualified substitute exists. A missing or illegible label makes the instrument unavailable until identity and status are verified.

## 3. Measurement capability and calibration acceptance

Calipers used for flange width must resolve 0.01 mm and show error no greater than ±0.02 mm at reference checks of 10, 40, and 80 mm. Angle fixtures must resolve 0.1 degree and show error no greater than ±0.1 degree at 89, 90, and 91 degrees. Pin-gauge certified diameter error must not exceed ±0.005 mm. Burr-comparator reference-step error must not exceed ±0.01 mm. These are instrument acceptance criteria, not additional product tolerances; AX-210 product acceptance remains governed by QUA-001.

For the 8.00 ± 0.10 mm hole requirement in QUA-001, the qualified 7.90 mm GO pin must pass through the hole with light hand pressure and the 8.10 mm NO-GO pin must not enter fully. Do not force a pin or use HP-4 to demonstrate fit. Ambiguous results require Quality review with a qualified alternate measurement method.

## 4. Daily verification and handling

Before first use each shift, inspect the instrument for damage, contamination, loose components, and battery faults. Clean contact surfaces using the approved gauge-care method. Stabilize instruments and brackets together for at least 20 minutes in the inspection area maintained at 20 ± 2 °C. If conditions fall outside that range, suspend acceptance measurement until conditions and stabilization are restored.

Verify caliper zero and the 40 mm reference, angle-fixture response at 90 degrees, pin identity and visible condition, and burr-comparator reference condition. Record actual reference readings where applicable, instrument identifier, reference identifier, time, and inspector role in QF-302. Apply the error limits in Section 3. Daily verification does not extend calibration dates. Dropped, overloaded, repaired, or visibly damaged gauges immediately receive a DO NOT USE tag, even if a quick check appears acceptable.

## 5. Traceability and external certificates

Calibration certificates must identify the instrument, method, reference standards, results before and after adjustment, measurement uncertainty, and traceability to recognized national or international measurement standards. The coordinator verifies certificate completeness and the provider's accredited scope before activating an instrument. A certificate that says only passed is insufficient. Missing as-found data requires an impact review before the gauge returns to service.

Link each QUA-001 inspection record to its actual instrument identifier. Retain certificates, verification logs, register history, and impact reviews for five years. Preserve superseded status records so investigators can reconstruct which gauge was available at a historical inspection time. Record corrections transparently; never overwrite a failed reading with a later passing value.

## 6. Failed verification and product impact

Notify the Metrology Coordinator immediately after failure, quarantine the gauge, and open QF-303. Quality places potentially affected product on hold under QUA-002 from the last documented successful verification through discovery, including shipped-product review. If that verification is missing or unreliable, extend the review to the last demonstrated valid calibration or earlier evidence-supported boundary.

Evaluate error direction, magnitude, measured characteristics, lot records, and uncertainty. Reinspect accessible affected brackets with a qualified independent gauge against QUA-001. A passing replacement measurement alone does not close the investigation: document affected quantities and unresolved shipment exposure. The Quality Manager approves the impact conclusion and any product release. Maintenance repair or calibration-provider adjustment cannot release held brackets.

## 7. Revision history

Revision 1 established the gauge register. Revision 2 added daily checks and certificate review. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, clarifies environmental stabilization, no overdue grace period, and product-impact linkage to QUA-001 and QUA-002.
