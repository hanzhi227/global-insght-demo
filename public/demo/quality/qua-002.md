# QUA-002 — Nonconformance Quarantine and Disposition

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01  
**Revision:** 3  
**Owner role:** Quality Engineer  
**Approval role:** Quality Manager  
**Controlled records:** QF-201 Nonconformance Report; QF-202 Hold Ledger

## 1. Scope and nonnegotiable boundaries

This procedure contains suspect AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets and controls decisions about rework, concession, return, or scrap. A hold is a prohibition on use or shipment, not a finding that every bracket is defective. Quality alone may release held product. Operations owns scheduling and material movement; Maintenance owns equipment checks; Safety owns isolation authorization and permits. No one may bypass guards, restart isolated equipment without authorized release, or falsify records to clear a backlog.

## 2. Initiating a hold

Any role discovering a defect, missing identity, mismatched label, incomplete inspection, or questionable measurement must stop transfer of the affected product and notify Quality immediately. Attach a red HOLD tag before the next movement. Identify the lot, container count, estimated bracket quantity, discovery time, discovery location, and reason. Do not mark an estimate as a verified count. Quality opens QF-201 and assigns a unique NCR identifier within 30 minutes of notification.

An AX-210 lot follows the heat, drawing, setup, and size definition in QUA-001. For a failed production checkpoint, initially contain all output since the last accepted checkpoint, including conveyor C-12 discharge, downstream finishing stock, and packed cartons. When that checkpoint cannot be established, hold the entire lot and all potentially related lots since the last verified conforming inspection. A suspect instrument may require broader containment under QUA-003.

## 3. Physical and electronic quarantine

Operations moves accessible material to the fenced quality quarantine area only through an authorized material-handling route. Each container receives its own HOLD tag linked to the same NCR. Quality sets the inventory status to quality hold and records bin location and transfer time in QF-202. Where material cannot safely be moved, establish a labeled in-place hold and prevent further allocation. Safety decides isolation or permit requirements; a hold tag is not an equipment isolation device.

Two roles, the Operations material coordinator and a Quality representative, reconcile physical count against the traveler and inventory balance before disposition. Investigate any mismatch rather than adjusting inventory to force agreement. The target is reconciliation within four hours of hold initiation. At shift handover, outgoing and incoming coordinators review every open container location and unresolved quantity.

## 4. Investigation and escalation

The Quality Engineer records the violated requirement, observed result, detection method, instrument identifier, and suspected extent. Product dimensions and sampling acceptance come from QUA-001; this procedure does not create alternate limits. Photographs may support the report but do not replace measurements or container identity. Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses such as press drift, coolant CL-7 staining, or air dryer AD-2 moisture carryover.

Request Maintenance assessment for equipment-related causes on HP-4, C-12, or AD-2. Maintenance supplies findings without authorizing product acceptance. Notify the Quality Manager within one hour when suspect product may have shipped, identities are lost, or three NCRs involve the same defect mechanism within 30 calendar days. For possible shipment escapes, Quality identifies customers and shipment references and controls notification; Operations suspends further shipment of the implicated scope.

## 5. Authorized dispositions

Quality must approve a written disposition before processing held brackets. Rework instructions define the affected quantity, approved method, required inspection, and routing. After rework, inspect every affected bracket against every characteristic changed or potentially affected by the operation, then complete the QUA-001 lot-release requirements. Sorting also examines every bracket for the identified defect and preserves accepted and rejected quantity reconciliation. Neither activity automatically releases product.

Use-as-is requires a documented concession reviewed by the Quality Manager and, when applicable, customer authorization. Record the exact requirement waived, lot, quantity, and expiration. No verbal approval or retrospective concession clears a shipment. Scrap decisions require Quality authorization and Operations confirmation that pieces are rendered unusable through the approved scrap route. Supplier returns remain held until the return transaction and physical departure are recorded.

## 6. Closure, exceptions, and performance

Quality signs the final product disposition and releases only the authorized quantity. QF-202 must reconcile original held quantity with released, scrapped, returned, and remaining held quantities. Partial release requires separate container identities; do not place mixed-status brackets in one carton. Close QF-201 only after records and labels agree. Retain NCR, hold, inspection, and concession records for five years.

If systems are unavailable, use sequentially numbered paper tags and ledger entries. Quality reconciles them before shipment. Never erase an entry: preserve the original and document correction reason, date, and authorizing role. The Quality Engineer reviews open holds daily; cases unresolved after two working days require a documented plan approved by the Quality Manager. Monthly review tracks reconciliation timeliness, repeat defects, and unauthorized movements, with a target of zero unauthorized releases.

## 7. Revision history

Revision 1 established quarantine tagging. Revision 2 added partial-release reconciliation and shipment-escape escalation. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, aligns containment boundaries, rework verification, and suspect-gauge investigations with QUA-001 and QUA-003.
