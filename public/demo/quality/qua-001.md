# QUA-001 — AX-210 Bracket Inspection and Release

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01  
**Revision:** 3  
**Owner role:** Quality Engineer  
**Approval role:** Quality Manager  
**Controlled records:** QF-101 Inspection Traveler; QF-102 Release Register

## 1. Purpose and authority

This procedure controls acceptance of AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets after forming and finishing. Acceptance means documented conformity, not permission to operate equipment. Quality alone may release held product. Operations owns scheduling and material flow; Maintenance owns equipment checks; Safety owns isolation authorization and permits. Never bypass guards, restart isolated equipment without authorized release, or falsify records. Production pressure, shipment commitments, and a successful machine check do not waive inspection.

## 2. Product criteria and lot definition

An inspection lot contains at most 500 brackets from one material heat, one drawing revision, and one uninterrupted forming setup on hydraulic press HP-4. A heat change, setup change, or shift boundary starts a new lot. Operations assigns a lot identifier before brackets leave the forming station. Mixed or unreadable identities require quarantine under QUA-002.

Controlled fictional acceptance limits are flange width 40.00 ± 0.20 mm, mounting-hole diameter 8.00 ± 0.10 mm, and formed angle 90.0 ± 0.5 degrees. Burr height must not exceed 0.10 mm. Reject any visible crack, missing hole, or corrosion. A cosmetic stain is acceptable only when it wipes away with the approved inspection wipe and leaves no pitting; do not authorize a cleaning chemistry through this procedure. Coolant CL-7 residue that prevents examination requires a hold pending an approved cleaning route.

## 3. Readiness and first-piece inspection

The inspector confirms traveler identity, current drawing, material certification reference, and instrument status under QUA-003. Record each instrument identifier, not merely its type. Calipers measure flange width, approved pin gauges assess hole size, the angle fixture measures bend angle, and the burr comparator assesses edges. Workpieces and instruments must satisfy QUA-003 environmental stabilization requirements.

Before normal production, inspect five consecutive first pieces for all listed characteristics. Record actual width and angle values, hole-gauge results, burr assessment, and appearance for each piece. All five must conform. A failure prevents first-piece approval and triggers QUA-002 containment. After an approved correction, inspect a fresh set of five; do not replace only the failed member of the original set. Quality signs first-piece approval before Operations moves accepted production into the next process queue.

## 4. Routine and final inspection

During production, inspect three consecutive pieces every 60 minutes or every 100 pieces, whichever occurs first. Inspect another three after any tooling adjustment or interruption that changes the setup. Measure all four controlled characteristics and examine appearance. Any failure holds production since the last accepted checkpoint, including downstream containers and work awaiting packaging. Operations locates the affected interval; Quality determines its final scope.

Final inspection samples 20 brackets distributed across the beginning, middle, and end of each lot. Inspect every bracket when the lot contains fewer than 20. The acceptance number is zero: one nonconforming result holds the entire lot under QUA-002. Sampling is not permission to knowingly ship a defect. Final inspection also verifies package count, lot label, drawing revision, and traveler completeness against the physical material.

## 5. Release decision and records

The inspector submits QF-101 with first-piece results, routine checkpoints, final results, gauge identifiers, and references to any rework or hold records. The Quality release authority checks completeness and signs QF-102 with lot identifier, accepted quantity, disposition references, date, and release time. Operations may stage material in a marked hold location before that signature but may not ship or merge it with released stock.

Electronic status and physical labels must agree. Where connectivity fails, use a sequentially numbered paper traveler and signed paper release entry; Quality reconciles both systems before shipment. Corrections preserve the original entry, include a reason and role signature, and never backdate an inspection. Retain completed inspection and release records for five years.

## 6. Escalation and exceptions

Notify the Quality Engineer immediately for failed samples, ambiguous drawings, missing certification, or an overdue gauge. Invoke QUA-002 if release evidence is absent or suspect. Notify Maintenance for unusual HP-4 behavior, conveyor C-12 damage, or air dryer AD-2 alarms; their equipment checks cannot release brackets. Safety governs access, isolation, and permits through the applicable controlled safety procedure. Operations reschedules work rather than bypassing a hold.

A customer concession must identify the exact lot, characteristic, quantity, and expiration and must be reviewed by the Quality Manager before any release. No verbal concession is valid. Unresolved identity, cracks, or absent authorization remain held.

## 7. Revision history

Revision 1 established first-piece and lot release controls. Revision 2 added checkpoint containment and electronic status reconciliation. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, aligns the zero-acceptance final sample, quarantine handoff, and gauge traceability with QUA-002 and QUA-003.
