## Inspection and deviations

The inspector records the batch identifier, inspection date, observed label defects, and the disposition decision. A batch with unreadable labels must be held for quality review. Only the designated quality owner may authorize its release.

If inspection results are missing, do not fabricate them. Record the missing inspection as a deviation and refer the batch to the quality owner.
