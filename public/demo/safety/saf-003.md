# SAF-003 — Contractor Access and Hot-Work Permits

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components — Cedar Falls Plant  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01  
**Revision:** 3  
**Owner role:** Plant Safety Lead  
**Approval role:** Plant Manager

## 1. Purpose and applicability

This document controls contractor entry and permits for welding, cutting, brazing, torch heating, spark-producing grinding, and comparable ignition-producing work at Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant. It applies to production and service areas supporting AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets, including work near conveyor C-12, hydraulic press HP-4, and air dryer AD-2. Calling a job a quick adjustment, repair ticket, vendor visit, or cold maintenance activity does not exempt ignition-producing work.

Safety owns access authorization and permits. Maintenance owns equipment checks and technical work acceptance. Operations owns scheduling and material flow. Quality alone releases held product. Never bypass guards, restart isolated equipment without authorized release, falsify records, or release held product outside Quality authority. C-12 isolation and release follow SAF-001. CL-7 releases follow SAF-002. This permit does not authorize equipment entry, chemical cleanup, or production release.

## 2. Contractor entry and role assignment

The sponsoring Maintenance or Operations role requests access with contractor organization, work scope, asset, planned location, required competencies, and expected dates. Safety verifies the contractor's relevant training evidence, site induction, emergency-route understanding, and task-specific hazard briefing before granting entry. Use role identifiers in work records; retain personal identifying information only in the separately controlled access system where required.

Each visit requires sign-in, a valid temporary badge, and sign-out. The badge expires at the end of the approved shift and is not transferable. An authorized site escort accompanies contractors until Safety documents the permitted unescorted work zone. Contractors may not enter adjacent production areas, photograph controlled processes, bring substitute workers, or change equipment scope without sponsor and Safety review. Visitors observing work remain outside the work boundary and do not become authorized workers by receiving a badge.

Safety identifies the Permit Issuer, contractor Work Lead, site Sponsor, and dedicated Fire Watch. The Fire Watch must be trained for the site response procedure, able to communicate immediately, and free of welding, material-handling, or other competing duties during the watch. Maintenance confirms technical compatibility; Operations confirms the location can be taken out of normal traffic.

## 3. Planning and prework inspection

The Permit Issuer and Work Lead walk the actual location before issuance. Review sparks, heat conduction, overhead deposits, wall penetrations, concealed spaces, nearby hoses, and opposite sides of surfaces receiving heat. Move combustible materials at least 11 meters from the ignition point. Where removal is impracticable, use approved noncombustible shielding and document its coverage; shielding does not excuse hidden combustible conditions that cannot be inspected.

Remove or protect affected AX-210 containers and establish a Quality hold for material exposed to uncertain contamination. Operations determines alternate material routes. Maintenance verifies equipment checks and applicable isolation requirements; the permit merely references that evidence. Do not duplicate or replace SAF-001 authorization with a contractor lock list.

No hot work is permitted during an active CL-7 spill response, with impaired fire protection, in a confined space under this permit alone, or where a potentially flammable atmosphere has not been cleared through the applicable specialist procedure. Aluminum fines or deposits require specific combustible-dust review; do not infer that solid brackets make nearby dust harmless. Safety refuses the permit when these conditions cannot be resolved.

## 4. Permit issuance and active control

Issue one permit for one location, one defined method, and one shift, with a maximum active duration of 8 hours. Record the asset, exact boundary, permitted tools, isolation reference where needed, shielding arrangements, extinguishing equipment inspection, Fire Watch role, start time, and expiry. Safety signs before the Work Lead begins. A work order or verbal schedule approval is not a permit.

Inspect the boundary before starting and after every interruption. A change of location, method, Work Lead, Fire Watch, ventilation condition, or nearby material cancels permission until Safety revalidates and documents the change. A break longer than 30 minutes requires a new prework inspection even if the permit remains within its original duration. Expiry stops ignition-producing work immediately.

The Fire Watch observes spark travel and adjacent spaces throughout work, keeps exits clear, and stops work upon smoke, uncontrolled sparks, shield displacement, unexpected personnel entry, or loss of communication. Raise the site alarm for fire or suspected hidden ignition; only trained personnel may attempt an incipient response within their site authorization and with a clear escape route. Contractors must not continue cutting while someone investigates smoke.

## 5. Completion and fire monitoring

After the final ignition-producing activity, maintain a continuous Fire Watch for 60 minutes. Inspect the work location, opposite surfaces, and adjacent accessible spaces at completion and at the end of that watch. Safety then assigns documented monitoring checks at 30-minute intervals for a further 120 minutes. Each check records time, role, inspected locations, and findings. Restarting hot work resets both periods from the new final activity.

Do not close the permit or leave the site without an accepted monitoring handover. Any heat, smoke, or suspicious odor triggers alarm evaluation and Safety escalation, not a routine completion signature. Maintenance accepts technical work independently; Safety closes the permit only after monitoring is complete. Operations resumes the affected route when Safety permits, equipment release is complete, and Quality has addressed held material.

## 6. Exceptions, records, and revision history

Unavailable monitoring coverage, impaired alarms, or schedule pressure cannot waive these controls. Escalate to the Plant Safety Lead for rescheduling. Retain access authorization references, induction evidence, permits, inspections, monitoring records, cancellations, and closeout for 3 years in the controlled Safety repository. Correct entries through attributable amendments.

Revision 1 introduced contractor permits. Revision 2 clarified independent equipment and product release. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, adds bounded permit duration, interruption inspection, explicit dust review, and documented postwork monitoring.
