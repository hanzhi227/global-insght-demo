# SAF-001 — C-12 Isolation and Restart Authorization

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components — Cedar Falls Plant  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01  
**Revision:** 3  
**Owner role:** Plant Safety Lead  
**Approval role:** Plant Manager

## 1. Purpose and controlled scope

This procedure controls authorization to isolate conveyor C-12 and permission to return it to service while producing AX-210 aluminum mounting brackets. Isolation means separating hazardous energy and securing each identified isolation point; a stopped belt, emergency-stop button, controller command, or operator assurance is not isolation. This document establishes Safety authorization, not an equipment repair method. Maintenance owns equipment checks and the equipment-specific energy inventory. Operations owns scheduling and material flow. Quality alone may release held product.

The universal invariant applies during breakdowns, planned work, and demonstrations: never bypass guards, never restart isolated equipment without authorized release, never falsify records, and never release held product outside Quality authority. Contractor access and ignition-producing work also require SAF-003. A CL-7 release requires SAF-002; neither document substitutes for this authorization.

## 2. Roles and permit conditions

The Safety Duty Officer issues the isolation authorization, identifies the work boundary, and approves release. A Maintenance Authorized Isolator applies the approved equipment-specific isolation plan and performs verification. Each exposed worker applies a personal lock through the designated group-lock arrangement before entering the danger zone. The Operations Shift Lead controls upstream feed and downstream accumulation without deciding whether equipment is safe. The Quality Duty Representative determines product containment and eventual disposition.

An authorization is valid for one defined work scope and no more than 12 hours. An unresolved job requires a new authorization before the previous one expires; expiry never removes locks or permits restart. A change in task, isolation points, adjacent equipment interaction, or responsible isolator requires Safety reassessment before work resumes. Maintenance must identify whether HP-4 or AD-2 introduces energy or motion into the work boundary; no assumed shared isolation is acceptable.

## 3. Initiating and establishing isolation

1. Operations stops feed, identifies the last known bracket lot and container locations, and requests an authorization with asset, reason, expected duration, and work description. If immediate danger exists, anyone may stop the process and withdraw; formal authorization follows before intervention.
2. Safety assigns an isolation number, identifies affected zones, and confirms access restrictions. Operations places affected AX-210 material on hold pending Quality review and redirects material flow only through an approved production route.
3. Maintenance reviews the current energy inventory at C-12, including electrical supply, pneumatic devices, stored belt tension, gravity-loaded components, and connected machinery. Missing labels or disagreement with the inventory stop the job and trigger escalation to the Maintenance Supervisor and Safety Duty Officer.
4. Maintenance shuts down using the approved method, opens identified energy-isolating devices, applies locks and tags, and dissipates or restrains stored energy using the equipment-specific method. Tags identify the authorization and role, not unnecessary personal information.
5. Before access, Maintenance verifies absence of hazardous energy using approved instruments and a controlled start attempt where applicable. Restore controls to their safe position afterward. Record each isolation point, verification method, instrument identifier where used, result, and time.
6. Safety checks that verification is complete and that each exposed worker has personal protection under the lock arrangement. Incomplete evidence means no entry, even when the belt appears motionless.

## 4. Work control and interruptions

Maintain a marked exclusion boundary around the affected conveyor section. Do not step over barriers to recover a dropped bracket or tool. Operations accounts for containers moved during isolation; Quality evaluates material exposed to repairs, debris, or uncertain traceability. Maintenance records findings separately from Safety authorization so a successful repair is not mistaken for restart permission.

At shift handover, outgoing and incoming authorized roles jointly review scope, energy points, workers, and outstanding defects. Incoming personal locks are established before outgoing protection is removed. If that overlap cannot be achieved, stop work and keep the established isolation secured. A temporary energization for testing requires withdrawal of all workers, closure of the current authorization through the release sequence, and a separately approved test boundary; re-entry requires fresh isolation and verification.

## 5. Release and controlled restart

Maintenance confirms the repair checks are complete, guards and protective devices are restored, loose items are removed, and the area is clear. Every worker accounts for their tools and removes their own personal lock only after leaving exposure. Safety reviews the lock register and verification record, then records explicit release authorization. Operations may request a start only after receiving that release.

Maintain the exclusion boundary through the initial start and observe C-12 for 5 minutes without introducing production material. Unexpected motion, abnormal sound, guard fault, or a missing person stops the restart and requires renewed isolation. Maintenance records observations; Safety confirms whether the authorization remains applicable. Operations resumes material flow only after the observation is complete. Trial brackets remain held until Quality releases them; mechanical readiness never releases product.

## 6. Exceptions, escalation, and records

An absent lock owner triggers escalation to the Plant Safety Lead and Plant Manager. Removal requires documented contact attempts, confirmation that the owner is not exposed, a physical area inspection, and approval by both roles. Notify the owner before their next work entry. Production urgency is not an exception.

Retain authorization, energy-point checklist, lock register, handover, restart observations, and exception approvals for 3 years in the controlled Safety repository. Correct errors by an attributable amendment preserving the original entry. Review any unauthorized start attempt before further operation.

## 7. Revision history

Revision 1 established authorization and individual protection. Revision 2 clarified Quality hold ownership. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, adds bounded authorization duration, shift overlap, controlled restart observation, and explicit abnormal-start escalation.
