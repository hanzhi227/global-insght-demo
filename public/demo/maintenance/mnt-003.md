# MNT-003 — AD-2 Compressed-Air Dryer Service

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01 | **Revision:** 3
**Owner:** Maintenance Supervisor | **Approval role:** Plant Engineering Manager
**Controlled scope:** Refrigerated compressed-air dryer AD-2 and its dedicated filters serving AX-210 aluminum mounting bracket production.

## 1. Purpose and interfaces

This instruction controls dryer condition monitoring, condensate removal verification, filter service, and moisture-fault diagnosis. AD-2 is a refrigerated dryer, not a desiccant tower; technicians must not apply regeneration instructions from another asset. Maintenance owns equipment checks. Operations owns service scheduling, downstream material flow, and coordinated demand reduction under OPS-001. Safety owns isolation authorization, permits, and authorized release under SAF-001. Quality owns product assessment and release under QUA-001.

No bypassing guards, no restarting isolated equipment without authorized release, and no falsifying records are permitted. Only Quality may release held product. Dryer acceptance verifies utility equipment condition, not bracket conformity. A maintenance clearance cannot remove a Quality hold caused by wet compressed air.

## 2. Schedule and measurable baseline

Read external indicators each operating shift and log inlet pressure, outlet pressure, pressure dew point, and drain status. Clean the accessible condenser face every 250 operating hours or 30 calendar days, whichever comes first. Service the automatic condensate drain and inspect dedicated filter bowls at the same interval under authorized isolation. Replace filter elements every 2,000 operating hours or 180 days, whichever comes first, or earlier when differential pressure exceeds its limit.

At the standard service observation condition, inlet pressure must be 6.5–8.0 bar, inlet air temperature no greater than 40°C, and ambient temperature 10–35°C. Pressure dew point must be no greater than +5°C after 15 minutes of stable operation. Dryer differential pressure must not exceed 0.35 bar; each dedicated filter must not exceed 0.20 bar. Use simultaneous upstream and downstream readings, not readings taken under different demand conditions. These are maintenance acceptance limits, not universal air-use specifications.

## 3. Preparation and isolated service

1. Review the previous seven shift readings and open work orders. Confirm the asset plate says AD-2. Obtain the specified filter elements, drain seals, and current utility schematic before beginning.
2. Ask Operations to schedule the outage and identify affected downstream users. Do not open an alternate air path as an informal workaround. Routing changes require the applicable controlled authorization and a documented Quality assessment when product exposure is possible.
3. Request Safety-controlled isolation, including the electrical supply and trapped pneumatic energy. Record the permit and authorization references. A zero indicator reading alone is not authority to remove a bowl.
4. Inspect the condenser face. Vacuum loose debris with equipment isolated; avoid bending fins or directing contamination into electrical components. A fin area visibly crushed across more than 10% of the face requires Engineering review because cleaning alone may not restore heat rejection.
5. Remove filter bowls only after the authorized isolation procedure is complete. Inspect for cracks, corrosion, damaged threads, and hardened seals. Reject any cracked bowl. Install the specified element and seal; record the element part number and retain its packaging identifier with the work order.
6. Clean the drain strainer and inspect the float or electronic level-sensing chamber according to the equipment procedure. Do not wire the drain open or defeat its level sensor. Route condensate to the approved collection point. Suspected CL-7 contamination requires investigation; condensate is not reusable coolant.

## 4. Return-to-service verification

Reassemble covers and filter bowls, account for tools, and request authorized release from isolation. Operations restores air demand in the agreed sequence. Maintenance observes from accessible external positions and records conditions after the 15-minute stabilization period. Confirm the automatic drain completes at least one observable discharge event during the verification period; if natural condensate is insufficient, use only the manufacturer's external test function and record that method.

Check connections using approved leak-detection solution. Expanding bubbles at any serviced joint require correction and repeat verification. Compare displayed dew point with a calibrated portable meter at the designated sampling port. A difference greater than 2°C requires instrument investigation; do not choose the more favorable reading. Accept maintenance clearance only when required pressure, temperature, dew-point, differential-pressure, leak, and drain checks pass.

## 5. Moisture faults and escalation

For high dew point, first verify inlet temperature, ambient condition, demand stability, and condenser cleanliness. High differential pressure with normal dew point suggests filter loading or restriction rather than refrigeration failure. Water downstream with an apparently normal dew point requires drain and sampling-location investigation; a display alone cannot disprove carryover.

Stop and escalate repeated compressor trips, unusual refrigerant-circuit noise, or suspected refrigerant leakage to the Maintenance Supervisor. Only an appropriately qualified refrigeration service provider may open the refrigeration circuit. Maintenance may not vent refrigerant or bridge a pressure switch. If conditions lie outside the service observation range, record the trial as inconclusive and coordinate a valid retest instead of declaring failure or success from unmatched readings.

## 6. Records, exceptions, and revision history

The work order captures hours, readings, demand condition, instrument identifiers, filter details, drain-test method, leak results, isolation and release references, and downstream notification. Missing instruments or unavailable replacement elements leave the affected task incomplete. Operations and Quality receive the equipment status and any suspected moisture-exposure window.

Revision 1 established dryer servicing. Revision 2 added differential-pressure trending. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, adds independent dew-point comparison, explicit drain verification, and separation of equipment clearance from product release.
