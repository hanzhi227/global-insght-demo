# MNT-002 — HP-4 Hydraulic Press Inspections

Fictional demo document — not approved for real plant operations.

**Company:** Northstar Precision Components, Cedar Falls Plant
**Effective date:** 2026-09-01 | **Revision:** 3
**Owner:** Maintenance Supervisor | **Approval role:** Plant Engineering Manager
**Controlled scope:** Hydraulic press HP-4 used in AX-210 aluminum mounting bracket production.

## 1. Purpose and ownership

This document defines mechanical and hydraulic condition inspection, sometimes called press-health verification or power-unit inspection. It does not establish bracket forming parameters, guarding authorization, or finished-product acceptance. Safety owns isolation authorization, permits, and authorized release under SAF-001. Operations owns inspection scheduling and material flow under OPS-001. Quality owns held-product disposition under QUA-001. Maintenance owns equipment checks, defect classification, and repair documentation. Only Quality may release held product. Never bypass guards, restart isolated equipment without authorized release, or falsify records.

The Maintenance Supervisor assigns a competent hydraulic technician and verifies that the latest HP-4 schematic is available. Operations removes workpieces when authorized and identifies the active tooling configuration. Tooling position can affect access and stored energy; a production stop is not isolation.

## 2. Frequency and inspection limits

Perform an external leak and condition inspection each operating shift. Complete the isolated hydraulic and mechanical inspection every 250 operating hours or 30 calendar days, whichever comes first. Sample reservoir oil every 1,000 operating hours or 90 days, whichever comes first, using the designated sampling port. After hose replacement, abnormal pressure loss, or unexpected ram movement, perform the affected inspection before returning the press to service.

Reservoir level must lie between the marked minimum and maximum with the ram in the documented service position. Hydraulic oil temperature must remain between 25°C and 55°C during the standard equipment trial. At the designated test connection, maintenance test pressure is 120 bar, acceptable from 117 to 123 bar. During the isolated, authorized diagnostic setup defined by the equipment service procedure, the pressure-hold test permits no more than 5 bar decay over 60 seconds. These values assess equipment condition; they are not authorized AX-210 production settings.

## 3. External survey and isolated inspection

1. Read the hour meter and review the previous inspection, open defects, and recent alarm history. Photograph equipment defects without including personal information. Record whether the press was cold, warmed, or recently stopped.
2. Observe hoses, reservoir seams, valve manifold, and cylinder connections from outside the safeguarded area. Any active drip, spray, mist, or growing wet patch requires stopping the equipment and notifying the supervisor. Never search for hydraulic pinhole leaks with a hand.
3. Request Safety authorization and the applicable stored-energy controls before opening access panels, touching hoses, or placing instruments. Record the isolation reference. Maintenance must not substitute a gauge reading for the Safety verification process.
4. Inspect each hose against its tag and routing drawing. Reject exposed reinforcement, blistering, a crushed section, damaged fittings, or rubbing that has penetrated the outer cover. Confirm clamps hold the hose clear of sharp edges throughout the documented motion envelope.
5. Inspect cylinder mounting hardware, ram guides, and accessible frame surfaces. A new crack indication, loose mounting fastener, or displaced witness mark requires supervisor review before maintenance clearance. Use specified torque values from the controlled equipment drawing, not improvised values.
6. Check reservoir level and contamination evidence. Milky oil, metallic particles, or an unexplained level increase requires sampling and engineering review. CL-7 coolant is not hydraulic fluid; suspected CL-7 ingress must be recorded as contamination and investigated, not corrected by topping up.

## 4. Instrumented checks and repair verification

Use a calibrated pressure gauge with a range covering the maintenance test pressure and a resolution of 1 bar or better. Fit it only through the authorized service arrangement. Record the gauge identifier and calibration due date. Do not crack a fitting to estimate pressure or release trapped energy.

Conduct the pressure-hold test only under the controlled diagnostic sequence and Safety authorization applicable to that setup. Record starting pressure, pressure at 60 seconds, oil temperature, and ram position. Excessive decay may indicate internal cylinder leakage, a valve seat defect, or a connection leak; do not declare the cylinder faulty without separating those possibilities through the equipment diagnostic procedure. Relief-valve adjustment requires Plant Engineering authorization and documented settings.

After repair, restore access covers and guards, account for tools, and obtain authorized release from isolation. Operations conducts five unloaded cycles using the approved service mode while Maintenance observes from a protected position. Accept the equipment trial only when temperature and pressure meet the listed limits, no new leakage appears, and motion is smooth without an unexplained pause. Product remains subject to Quality disposition.

## 5. Exceptions, escalation, and records

Stop testing if instrumentation is damaged, a service procedure is missing, or the required protected observation position cannot be maintained. Record the check as incomplete and escalate to the Maintenance Supervisor. Repeated seal failure within 30 days requires Engineering root-cause review before another like-for-like replacement is accepted as the complete corrective action.

Record meter hours, hose tags, defect locations, fluid observations, instrument identifiers, pressure decay, temperature, parts, repair authorization, isolation release reference, and trial outcome. Link any potentially affected AX-210 lot to its Quality hold record. Never label a blank field as passed or backdate an inspection.

## 6. Revision history

Revision 1 introduced hydraulic inspections. Revision 2 added oil sampling and hose traceability. Revision 3, effective 2026-09-01, distinguishes maintenance test pressure from production settings and clarifies controlled diagnostic authorization and product-release boundaries.
