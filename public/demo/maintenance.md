# Fictional maintenance manual — conveyor C-12

DEMO ONLY. This is synthetic text, not an approved plant manual.

## Inspection record

During a scheduled inspection, the authorized technician records the conveyor identifier, inspection date, guard condition, observed belt damage, and any unusual noise. Record findings before requesting restart approval.

## Restart approval

After a maintenance intervention, the supervisor checks that the inspection record is complete and that authorized personnel have confirmed readiness. Safety prerequisites are in the separate C-12 safety procedure.

This demo does not specify belt tension settings, lubrication intervals, electrical troubleshooting steps, or procedures for other equipment.
